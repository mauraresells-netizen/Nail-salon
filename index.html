Our Story

Crafted in Glasgow.
Inspired by Art.

Born and raised in Glasgow, our studio began with a simple belief — that nail care, done beautifully, is one of life's quiet luxuries. We opened our doors in the heart of the city to bring something a little more considered to the everyday.

Our founding nail artist trained in London and Paris, perfecting her craft in some of the most celebrated nail bars in Europe, before bringing that artistry home to Glasgow. Every treatment is delivered with the same precision, patience and care.

We use only premium, professional products and create a calm, indulgent space where you can truly switch off. Because nail care is self-care — and every client deserves to leave feeling extraordinary.
